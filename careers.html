Careers

Six open roles across three cities. Every one has a pay band printed below, because an advert without a number wastes your afternoon and ours.

How we hire

Read the role, email the address at the bottom of it, and tell us in a few paragraphs what you have cooked or served and what you want to learn next. We do not need a designed resume and we do not use an application tracking system. A person reads every email, usually within four working days.

If it looks like a fit, the process is a phone call, then a paid trial shift at your normal hourly rate, then a decision within forty eight hours of the trial. We pay for trials because working for free is how this industry keeps people out of it. We will tell you no clearly rather than going quiet.

Pay bands are for the role as advertised. Where a role earns a share of the service charge, that is stated separately and is not counted inside the band.
