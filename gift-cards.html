The Cellar Club

Free to join, no card to carry, and no requirement to give us anything beyond an email address and the name on your reservation.

You earn one point for every dollar you spend on food and drink at any of the three restaurants, on Noir at Home orders placed through this site, and in the Pantry. Two hundred and fifty points becomes a twenty five dollar credit, which is a straight ten percent return. Points do not expire while your account is active, and an account stays active as long as you visit once every twenty four months.

Third party delivery orders do not earn points, because those platforms do not pass us the information we would need to credit you. That is a limitation, not a policy.

  • CellarFrom your first visit

    • One point per dollar, 250 points becomes a $25 credit
    • Menu letter six times a year if you want it

  • ReserveAt $2,500 a year

    • Priority access to the reservation release, forty eight hours early
    • One complimentary cheese course per visit
    • Invitation to the two annual cellar dinners at cost

  • MagnumAt $7,500 a year

    • A held table at any location with twenty four hours notice
    • First refusal on chef’s table cancellations
    • One Salle Noire booking a year with the minimum spend waived
