If something went wrong

Tell the manager on duty while you are still in the room if you possibly can. Almost everything is fixable in the moment and nothing is fixable a week later. If you would rather not, or if it only occurred to you afterwards, write to hello@maisonnoir.com and Tobias Lindqvist will read it himself.

We do not ask for reviews, we do not offer anything in exchange for one, and we never write our own. If you see a review claiming to be from us, it is not.
