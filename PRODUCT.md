# PRODUCT.md — Maison Noir

## What this is
A multi-page marketing and transaction-adjacent website for **Maison Noir**, a
tasting-menu restaurant group with three US locations (New York, Washington DC,
San Francisco). Static HTML5 + one CSS file + one vanilla JS file. No build step,
no framework, no CDN CSS framework.

**Register:** brand. The design *is* the product on this site: the page has to
feel like the dining room before anyone reads a price.

## Audience
USA diners, 30-65, booking an occasion 2-6 weeks out. Secondary: corporate event
planners sourcing private dining, and gift-card buyers. Mobile is the majority
device for discovery; desktop dominates for the reservation and catering forms.

## Business model (must be legible on the site)
1. **Dine-in** tasting menus with reservation tiers (dining room, kitchen
   counter, chef's table) and a 14-seat private room, the Salle Noire.
2. **Takeout + delivery** — first-party "Noir at Home" ordering plus three
   third-party marketplaces, with the price difference disclosed honestly.
3. **Catering and private events** with published per-guest package pricing.
4. **Gift cards** (no expiry, no fees) and the **Cellar Club** loyalty program.
5. **Retail** — the Noir Pantry: sauces, coffee beans, salt, cookbook, apron.
6. **Chef's Table / tasting menu** as the premium tier.

No hidden fees anywhere. Every charge that will appear on a guest's bill is
stated on the page that sells the thing.

## Non-negotiables
- WCAG 2.1 AA. Skip link, landmarks, visible focus, keyboard operable overlay
  nav, alt text on every image, 4.5:1 body contrast, reduced-motion honored.
- Google AdSense policy compliance: substantial original copy on every page, no
  dead links, full legal set, cookie consent before non-essential cookies,
  labeled ad slots that never sit above the fold and never appear on legal pages
  or the 404.
- Every image is a real hosted photograph (Unsplash direct URLs, all verified
  200). Zero SVG artwork anywhere in the project.
- Prices in USD, phone numbers in `+1 (xxx) xxx-xxxx`, real US street addresses.

## Honesty constraints written into the copy
- No Michelin star claim, no award claim, no press quote attributed to a real
  publication. The restaurant is fictional; the site never implies otherwise by
  borrowing a real institution's credibility.
- Phone numbers use the reserved 555-01xx fictional range on purpose.
- No fake scarcity, no countdown, no invented testimonials.
