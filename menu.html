The Menu

The dining room serves tasting menus only. Everything on the à la carte list below is served at Bar Noir and in the lounge, where you can eat one dish or seven. All prices are in US dollars.

Tasting menus

One menu per table. We ask that the whole table takes the same length of menu so the kitchen can pace the room.

Dining room

Priced per guest

Tax and any beverages are added to your bill. No service charge for parties of five or fewer.

  • Le Noir$255

    • Nine courses drawn from the day's market, about two and a half hours
    • Served Tuesday through Sunday at every location
    • Wine pairing $165, reserve pairing $310, zero proof pairing $65

  • Le Court$165

    • Five courses, the same cooking at a shorter length, about ninety minutes
    • Tuesday through Thursday and Sunday, first two seatings only
    • Wine pairing $95

  • Vegetal Noir$235

    • Nine plant based courses cooked on a dedicated station, not a substitution menu
    • Available at every seating with twenty four hours notice
    VGNotice required

  • Chef's Table$395

    • Fourteen to sixteen courses at the kitchen counter, Tribeca only, eight seats
    • Includes the trimmings course and the fermentation flight
    • Three and a half hours, one seating per night at 6:15 PM

Reading the tags

Four tags appear beside dishes on this page. They describe the dish as written, before any modification.

GFVVGN

  • GFNo gluten ingredient

  • VVegetarian

  • VGVegan

  • NContains tree nuts

Our kitchens handle gluten, dairy, egg, soy, sesame, shellfish, fish and tree nuts every service, so we cannot promise any dish is free of traces. Please read the allergen and nutrition disclaimer and tell your server about every allergy before you order.
