Private Events

A fourteen seat room behind an oak door, the whole restaurant if you want it, or our kitchen in yours. Every minimum and every charge is published below.

The Salle Noire

A single oak table for fourteen in a room with its own service door, its own sound system and nine years of framed menus along the corridor outside. It is a private room, not a curtained corner of the dining room, and it has a dedicated server and a dedicated section of the kitchen for the length of your booking.

Bookings run three and a half hours from the seating time. There is no room rental fee. Instead there is a minimum spend on food and beverage, which is lower early in the week because that is when we need the business.

  • Friday and Saturday minimum$6,500

    • Food and beverage combined, before tax and service charge
    • One seating per night, either 5:30 PM or 8:30 PM

  • Tuesday to Thursday and Sunday minimum$4,200

    • Same room, same team, same menu

  • Room deposit$1,000

    • Charged at booking and credited against the final bill
    • Refundable up to fourteen days before the date

  • Service charge22% on the final bill

    • Printed on your invoice and distributed in full to the team working the room
    • This replaces tipping. Nothing further is expected

  • Screen and audioNo charge

    • A 65 inch display, HDMI and USB-C, and a hard wired sound input
    • Tell us in advance and we will test it with your laptop before guests arrive
