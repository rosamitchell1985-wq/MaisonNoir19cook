4. Reservations and deposits

Submitting the reservation form sends a request. A reservation exists only once we confirm it by email. Deposits are as follows and are credited in full against your final bill rather than charged in addition to it:

  • Dining room: fifty dollars per guest.
  • Chef's table: one hundred and fifty dollars per guest.
  • Bar Noir and lounge: no deposit.
  • Salle Noire private room: a one thousand dollar room deposit.

Cancel or amend more than forty eight hours before your seating and the deposit is released in full. Inside forty eight hours we retain the deposit for the seats we cannot resell. Chef's table deposits are non refundable inside seventy two hours because the kitchen buys specifically for those eight seats. Reducing a party size inside forty eight hours retains the deposit on the removed seats. We hold your table for twenty minutes past the seating time, after which the kitchen may serve a shortened menu at the full price.

A twenty percent service charge is added to parties of six or more and is shown on your check. It is distributed in full to the kitchen and service teams. Sales tax is added at the applicable local rate.

5. Online orders

Submitting the order form sends a request. We call or text to confirm the window and to take payment, and the order is accepted at that point. If we cannot fulfil an item we will tell you before taking payment and you may cancel without charge.

Because prepared food is perishable, orders cannot be cancelled once preparation has started. If something is wrong with your order, tell us within twenty four hours and we will replace it or refund it. Our own delivery charge is six dollars and ninety five cents, waived on orders over eighty five dollars within three miles. There is no service fee, packaging fee, small order fee or card surcharge on a first party order.

Orders placed through DoorDash, Uber Eats or Caviar are governed by that platform's terms as well as these, and their menu prices are higher than ours because they charge us a commission.

6. Gift cards

Gift cards are available from fifty to two thousand dollars. They do not expire, the balance does not reduce over time, and there is no activation fee, dormancy fee or reissue fee. They are redeemable at all three restaurants, at Bar Noir, against Noir at Home orders placed on this site, and in the Noir Pantry. They are not redeemable on third party delivery platforms.

Gift cards are not reloadable and cannot be exchanged for cash except where state law requires it, which we will honour on request. Unused cards may be refunded in full to the original payment method within thirty days of purchase. Lost or destroyed cards are replaced free of charge if you can give us the original order number. We are not responsible for gift cards bought from anyone other than us, and cards obtained through unauthorised resale may be voided.

7. The Cellar Club

Membership is free and voluntary. You earn one point per dollar spent on food and beverage at our restaurants, on first party online orders and in the Pantry. Two hundred and fifty points converts to a twenty five dollar credit. Points have no cash value, cannot be sold or transferred, and are forfeited if an account is inactive for twenty four months. Third party delivery orders do not earn points because those platforms do not pass us the information needed to credit them. We may change the earn or redemption rate with thirty days notice by email, and existing unredeemed points will be honoured at the old rate for those thirty days.

We will never add a charge to an event invoice that was not on the signed quote.
