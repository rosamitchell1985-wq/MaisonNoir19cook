Cookie Policy

Last updated August 17, 2026

Nothing optional is stored on your device until you tell us it can be. This page lists everything we use and how to change your mind.

What a cookie is, briefly

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to keep. Similar technologies include local storage, which works the same way from your point of view. This policy covers both, and we use the word "cookie" for all of them.

Your choices, and how we honour them

On your first visit a banner appears at the bottom of the page. It does not cover the content, it does not appear as a full screen interstitial, and it offers three real choices:

  • Accept all. Strictly necessary, analytics and advertising.
  • Reject optional. Strictly necessary only. This is a single click and it is not hidden behind a second screen.
  • Manage preferences. Turn analytics and advertising on or off independently, then save.

Until you choose, we set nothing except the record of your choice once you make it. No analytics script and no advertising script is loaded before consent. You can change your decision whenever you like using the Cookie Preferences button in the footer of every page, and you can withdraw a consent as easily as you gave it.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage will not break the site, but it means your cookie choice cannot be remembered, so the banner will appear again on every visit.

Category one: strictly necessary

These make the site work. They cannot be switched off in our banner because without them we cannot honour your choice or keep a form usable.

  • mn_consent_v112 months

    • Local storage. Records which cookie categories you allowed and when
    • Set by us, first party, never shared

  • Form stateUntil the tab closes

    • Session storage. Keeps what you typed if a page reloads mid form
    • Set by us, first party

  • Load balancingSession

    • Set by our hosting provider so your requests reach a consistent server

Category two: analytics

Off unless you turn it on. These tell us in aggregate which pages people read and where they leave, which is how we decided to publish the Journal and to put prices on every page. We do not use analytics to identify you personally and we do not combine it with your reservation record.

  • _ga2 years

    • Google Analytics. Distinguishes one browser from another
    • Third party, set by Google, only after you consent

  • _ga_<container>2 years

    • Google Analytics. Maintains session state for our property

  • _gid24 hours

    • Google Analytics. Distinguishes visitors within a day

Category three: advertising

Off unless you turn it on, and off by default in the Manage preferences panel. Some pages on this site carry clearly labelled third party advertising. When you allow this category, Google and other vendors may set cookies to select and measure those advertisements.

Google, as a third party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Its use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet, including through the DoubleClick DART cookie. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and from many other vendors at aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative. Refusing this category in our banner prevents these cookies being set here at all, which is the more complete option.

  • IDEUp to 13 months

    • Google advertising. Ad selection, delivery and measurement
    • Third party, only after you consent to advertising

  • DSID2 weeks

    • Google advertising. Links activity across devices for signed in users

  • test_cookie15 minutes

    • Google advertising. Checks whether your browser accepts cookies

  • __gads13 months

    • Google advertising. Frequency capping and ad measurement

What we do not use

No social media tracking pixels. No advertising cookies from platforms we do not name on this page. No fingerprinting, no session recording, no heat mapping and no cross site tracking of our own. We do not sell the data any of these cookies produce.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Browsers send Do Not Track inconsistently and there is no agreed standard for honouring it, so we do not rely on it. We do respect the Global Privacy Control signal where your browser or extension sends one: it is treated as a request to opt out of advertising, and the advertising category will stay off.

Changes and questions

If we add or remove a cookie we will update this page and change the date at the top. Where a new cookie falls into a category you have already refused, it will not be set until you change that preference. Questions go to privacy@maisonnoir.com or to Privacy Request, Maison Noir Hospitality LLC, 118 Hudson Street, Suite 2, New York, NY 10013.
